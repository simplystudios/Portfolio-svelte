# Gist

![A screenshot of the app](gistcover.jpg)

Problem with most transit apps is that they suck bluntly. They look like they were designed for 80-year-olds, the whole design is bad, and they have never followed a uniform design with elegant colors.

So I decided if I am gonna travel in the metro, I'll do it my way. That's when the idea came to the idea board.

## The Solution
Instead of cluttered menus, I focused on:
* **Design:** Clean, minimalist, and dark-mode first.
* **Functionality:** Fast pathfinding using custom algorithms.
* **Flow:** Zero unnecessary taps to get your route.

[Check out the Live App ↗](https://detroweb.vercel.app)
